TEXTBOOK / CONCEPTUAL ALIGNMENT
-------------------------------
The course follows the standard Chapter 1 structure of:
  Rosen, Kenneth H. "Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications,"
  McGraw-Hill (multiple editions, most recently 8th ed., 2018).
  ISBN-13: 978-1259676512.

Definitions of even/odd integers, rational numbers, prime numbers, the
Hardy-Ramanujan number 1729, the irrationality of √2 (Pythagorean
school), and the infinitude of primes (Euclid, Elements Book IX,
Proposition 20) are classical results from the standard mathematical
canon and appear in every introductory discrete math textbook,
including Rosen.

The De Morgan laws are named after Augustus De Morgan (1806-1871).
The 'knights and knaves' puzzle in Section 1.2 is from Raymond
Smullyan, "What Is the Name of This Book?" (Prentice-Hall, 1978).
