               Definition:
                       In very basic terms, a summation is the addition of a
                           set of numbers.
               Example:
                       Let’s say we want to sum the integers from 1 to 5
                       Here is how you write this out as a summation:
                             5

                           ∑ i = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15
                            i =1

                       Here, the “i” is just a variable.

                       Again, we need to determine, for the “stuff”
                           inside the summation, what are constants and
                           what are variables.
                                  We know 42 is a constant.
                                  What about j?

                                  j is also a constant!

                                  Why?

                                  The only time that letters (inside the summation) count

                                   as variables are when they are the EXACT SAME
                                   letter as the index of the summation
