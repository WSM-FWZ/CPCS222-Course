# CPCS 222 — Discrete Mathematics — Course Outline

Source: 24 PDFs in `/contents/` (lecture slides + lab handouts + assessments).
Course follows the structure of Rosen's *Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications*.

### Chapter 2 — Basic Structures: Sets
- Set notation, set-builder, Venn diagrams
- Subsets, power sets, Cartesian products
- Set operations (union, intersection, difference, complement, symmetric difference)
- Set identities and proofs

Lecture slides: `CPCS222_04_Sets.pdf` · Lab: 07

### Chapter 3 — Sequences and Summations
- Sequences, recurrence relations, special integer sequences
- Summations (finite, infinite, telescoping, geometric)
- Cardinality and countable sets

Lecture slides: `CPCS222_05_Sequences.pdf`, `CPCS222_06_Summations.pdf` · Lab: 08

### Chapter 4 — Induction (Mathematical Induction & Recursion)
Lecture slides: `CPCS222_MathematicalInduction.pdf`

### Chapter 5 — Functions
- Domain, codomain, range
- Injective, surjective, bijective
- Inverse and composition
- Floor and ceiling

Lecture slides: `CPCS222_07_Functions.pdf` · Lab: 09

### Chapter 6 — Relations
- Relations on sets, n-ary relations
- Properties (reflexive, symmetric, transitive, antisymmetric)
- Equivalence relations and partitions
- Partial and total orders, Hasse diagrams

Lecture slides: `CPCS222_08_Relations.pdf` · Lab: 10

### Chapter 7 — Counting
- Basic rules (sum, product, subtraction, division)
- Pigeonhole principle
- Permutations and combinations
- Binomial theorem
- Generalized permutations/combinations

Lecture slides: `CPCS222_Counting.pdf` · Labs: 11, 12
